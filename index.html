      How to use this app

      1. Scan live, take a barcode photo, or type the barcode.
      2. Read the green, amber or red reason. Correct missing label details when needed.
      3. Save the product to My food or add it to Shopping.
      4. Use plus/minus as stock changes. Low stock moves to Shopping automatically.
      5. Create meals from saved food; cooking deducts stock.
      6. Enter Coles and Woolworths prices to see the cheaper split.
      7. Use More for diary notes, settings and backups.

      Important safety note

      This personal tool helps organise label information. Packaging can change. Always read the current label and follow advice from your treating health professionals. Amber means the app cannot confirm safety.
