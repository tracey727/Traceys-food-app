HOW TO USE GENEVIEVE APP™ FOOD V24 FIXED

1. Open Scan.
2. Take or choose the front, ingredients and allergen photos. Each photo must show a preview immediately.
3. Press READ ALL PHOTOS + LOAD PRODUCT.
4. On the result page, check the loaded name, barcode, ingredients and allergens.
5. Correct anything missed, tick the physical-label confirmation only after checking the current packet, then press USE THESE UPDATED DETAILS + RE-CHECK SAFETY.
6. Press SAVE TO MY FOOD / STOCK to keep the product and quantity controls.

For barcode 4901515129889, the app loads Kikkoman Naturally Brewed Gluten Free Soy Sauce and does not invent an oat warning from poor OCR.

This is a personal safety aid, not a substitute for checking the current package or professional medical advice.
